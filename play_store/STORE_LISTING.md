## 📄 Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

```markdown
SmartVision is the official AI-powered biometric attendance and academic portal designed exclusively for Idea Lab at JIET Jodhpur. Built on edge deep neural networks and seamless DigiCampus ERP integration, SmartVision eliminates manual registers, biometric fingerprint queues, and proxy attendance.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

1. CONTACTLESS AI FACE RECOGNITION:
• High-speed, edge facial detection powered by deep learning models.
• Real-time attendance logging with instant audio-visual confirmation.
• Anti-spoofing and cooldown window to prevent accidental duplicate entries.

2. DIGICAMPUS ERP REVERSE-ENGINEERED EXPERIENCE:
• Academics Overview: View all enrolled semester courses (Exploratory Project, Engineering Chemistry, Soft Skills, Programming in C, Professional Skills, Sports for Life) at a glance.
• Attendance Percentage Bars: Real-time calculation of classes attended vs classes held, with visual threshold indicators.
• Detailed Session Ledger: Chronological date-wise breakdown showing Slot 1 (01:20 PM) and Slot 2 (02:10 PM) statuses with green (Present), red (Absent), and gray (Not marked) badges.

3. IDEA LAB 2-SLOT LAB ATTENDANCE:
• Automatically tracks the mandatory 50% lab attendance requirement.
• Instant shortage warnings and dynamic calculation of sessions needed to restore good standing.
• Transparent 6:00 PM audit lock ensuring institutional data integrity.

4. 100% DPDP & GDPR PRIVACY COMPLIANT:
• Built with privacy-by-design principles under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and GDPR.
• Facial templates are stored as secure, non-reversible 128-dimensional mathematical embeddings.
• Zero third-party ad tracking or commercial data sharing.
• "Right to be Forgotten": Submit an in-app biometric data erasure request anytime with permanent 48-hour purge processing.

5. SEAMLESS MOBILE APP EXPERIENCE:
• Instant push notifications for attendance marks.
• Offline caching with smart sync when internet connectivity restores.
• Fast pull-to-refresh and native camera permissions for profile enrollment.

Designed for Idea Lab Students, Faculty, and Institutional Administrators.
Institutional Support: idealab@jietjodhpur.ac.in
Grievance Officer: grievance-idealab@jietjodhpur.ac.in
```
