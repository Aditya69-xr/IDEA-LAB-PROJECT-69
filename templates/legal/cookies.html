Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Strictly essential cookies only • Zero third-party advertising trackers

SmartVision respects user privacy. We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.

1. Strictly Essential Cookies

These cookies are technically required for the security and fundamental operation of the web application:

Cookie Name Type Purpose Lifespan
session Essential / Secure Cryptographically signed session cookie maintaining user authentication and preventing CSRF attacks. Configured with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. 12 Hours / Browser Session
2. Browser Local Storage

We utilize local browser storage (`localStorage`) strictly to remember your interface preferences on your device:

  • sv_cookie_consent: Stores your cookie banner acknowledgement.
  • sv_audio_muted: Stores your preference for entrance chime audio feedback.
3. No Third-Party Tracking

SmartVision does not partner with ad networks or tracking brokers. All content security policies strictly block non-essential third-party connections.
