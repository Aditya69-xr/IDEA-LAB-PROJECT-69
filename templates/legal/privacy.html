Biometric Privacy Policy

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 & GDPR

SmartVision is committed to the highest standards of data security, transparency, and personal privacy for all members, students, researchers, and faculty of the Idea Lab.

1. What Data We Collect

SmartVision adheres strictly to the principle of Data Minimization. We collect only what is necessary to operate automated lab attendance:

  • Full Name & Student / Member ID: For academic credential identification.
  • Department & Role: To classify membership and generate lab reports.
  • 128-Dimensional Mathematical Face Embedding: When your photo is captured or uploaded, our deep neural network (SFace) transforms the image into an irreversible 128-float mathematical vector. Raw faces are processed into numerical embeddings.
  • Check-in Timestamps & Verification Snapshots: Date, time, and confidence score logged upon passing through the entrance camera.
2. On-Premise Local Storage & Zero Cloud Leaks

Unlike commercial cloud surveillance systems:

  • All biometric vectors and attendance records are stored exclusively in an on-premise local SQLite database residing on the Idea Lab controller workstation.
  • Your biometric embeddings are never sold, rented, monetized, or shared with third-party advertisers, cloud APIs, or analytics vendors.
3. Security Measures

We enforce multi-tiered defense against unauthorized access:

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Only authorized administrators with cryptographically hashed passwords can access the central directory.
  • Zero IDOR Isolation: Students logging in can strictly view only their own personal attendance record and cannot access other students' files.
  • OWASP Defenses: Protected by rate-limiting against brute force, input sanitization against XSS/SQL injection, and secure HTTP session flags (`HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`).
4. Right to be Forgotten (Data Erasure)

Under Section 12 of the DPDP Act and Article 17 of the GDPR, you have the fundamental right to withdraw consent and request total erasure of your biometric data at any time:

  • Students may submit an instant erasure request directly through their Student Portal.
  • Upon confirmation, all facial templates, enrolled portrait photos, and snapshot history are permanently purged from disk and database.
