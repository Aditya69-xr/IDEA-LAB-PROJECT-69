Refund & Fee Transparency Policy

Clear, 100% transparent terms with zero hidden fees

SmartVision and the Idea Lab operate with absolute fee transparency. We have zero dark patterns, no recurring auto-renewing hidden charges, and clear refund timelines.

1. Core Attendance Service is Free

Attendance verification, facial enrollment, student portal access, and individual attendance tracking are provided 100% free of charge as an institutional academic service for all Idea Lab members.

2. Consumable Materials & Hardware Kit Deposits

Where lab members opt to check out specialized physical prototyping components (e.g. Arduino kits, microcontrollers, filament, sensors, or specialized tooling):

  • Security Deposits: 100% refundable upon safe return of undamaged hardware kits at the end of the lab term.
  • Unused Consumables: If a student withdraws from a specialized project track within 14 days before materials are milled or printed, all unused consumable lab fees are refunded within 7 business days.
3. How to Request a Refund

To request deposit return or fee adjustment, email the Lab Accounts Coordinator at idealab@college.edu with your Student ID and receipt number. All requests are processed within 5 to 7 banking days directly to the original bank account.
