Idea Lab Biometric Attendance & Laboratory Facility Governance

By enrolling in the Idea Lab SmartVision AI Attendance System, you agree to adhere to the academic standards, safety guidelines, and code of conduct set forth below.

1. Strict Zero Proxy Attendance Policy

SmartVision is designed to uphold academic integrity. Any attempt to defeat or spoof the biometric facial recognition system—including but not limited to holding up printed 2D photos, digital screens, silicone masks, or identity spoofing—constitutes an academic honor code violation and will result in disciplinary action and debarment from the Idea Lab.

2. Attendance Qualification Standards

In accordance with institutional guidelines, all enrolled lab researchers and students are required to maintain a minimum of 75.0% cumulative attendance across all scheduled academic lab sessions to be eligible for end-of-semester lab credit and project certification.

3. Laboratory Equipment & Facility Care

Members granted access to Idea Lab 3D printers, laser cutters, electronics benches, and Arduino/IoT prototyping kits must handle equipment with due care and report any hardware malfunctions to lab administrators immediately.

4. Account Security

Students are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their Student Portal passwords. Sharing portal credentials or attempting to access other members' records violates these Terms.

Questions or Appeal? For attendance disputes or technical discrepancies, submit an audit request to your Idea Lab instructor or contact idealab@college.edu.
