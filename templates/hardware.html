Step-by-Step Setup Instructions:
  1. Connect Green LED Anode (+) to Digital Pin 12 through a 220Ω resistor.
  2. Connect Red LED Anode (+) to Digital Pin 11 through a 220Ω resistor.
  3. Connect Buzzer (+) to Digital Pin 8.
  4. Connect all LED Cathodes (-) and Buzzer (-) to the common GND pin.
  5. Upload the provided sketch: arduino/smartvision_arduino.ino using Arduino IDE.
  6. Plug USB cable into laptop, select port above, and click Connect!
